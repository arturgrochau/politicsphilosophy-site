One argument at a time

Six essays, each around fifteen hundred words, each ending on the criticism its subject never answered.

c. 150 BC · Greek

Polybius and anakyklosis, the cycle of constitutions

Every simple regime rots into its own worst version, in a fixed order. Rome held the cycle off for a while by mixing all three at once.

Polybius, Histories VI 1377 · Tunis and Cairo

Ibn Khaldun on why dynasties die in four generations

Political power rests on group feeling forged by hardship. Cities dissolve it, comfort finishes the job, and a dynasty gets about four generations.

Ibn Khaldun, Muqaddimah c. 1517 · Florence

Machiavelli and the ten-year cure for corruzione

Rome stayed free because its classes fought in public. Machiavelli read conflict as maintenance rather than disease, which nobody else was willing to say.

Machiavelli, Discourses on Livy 1918 · Munich

Oswald Spengler on why political orders decay

The most famous decline book of the last century, and the one to handle with tongs. What the morphology claimed, why specialists rejected it, and who picked it up afterwards.

Spengler, The Decline of the West 1930 · Madrid

Ortega y Gasset and the hombre-masa

Mass-man is a temperament rather than a class: the person who enjoys a civilisation without asking what maintains it, and who is found in the professions as often as anywhere.

Ortega y Gasset, The Revolt of the Masses 1988 · Archaeology

Joseph Tainter and the cost of complexity

Complexity is a problem-solving strategy with declining marginal returns. When they go negative, collapse stops being a catastrophe and becomes the rational option.

Tainter, The Collapse of Complex Societies
What this is

The writing arm of a Discord that argues about this nightly

Politics & Philosophy has been running since March 2019 and now has around 1,200 members. A chat window is a bad place for a long argument, so the long ones live here.

Every date and title on this site is checked against primary texts and standard reference works before it goes up, and the About page explains exactly how, including what is machine-assisted. The glossary covers the vocabulary, from asabiyya to elite overproduction.
